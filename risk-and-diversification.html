Risk and diversification

Investing always involves uncertainty. Understanding risk can help people make more thoughtful choices.

Prices can go down

A company may face competition, changing costs, or lower demand. Broader events can also affect many companies at once. You could lose some or all of the money invested in a stock.

What diversification means

Diversification means spreading investments across different companies, industries, or asset types instead of relying on only one. If one investment performs poorly, others may behave differently.

Diversification has limits

Spreading investments can reduce the effect of problems in a single company, but it cannot remove all risk or guarantee a gain. Time horizon, goals, and personal circumstances matter.

Remember Diversification can help spread some risks, but no strategy guarantees a profit or prevents every loss.
