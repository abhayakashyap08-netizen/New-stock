Stock basics

A stock share represents a small piece of ownership in a company.

What does ownership mean?

Companies can divide ownership into shares. When someone owns a share, they own a small portion of the company. The share does not mean they can use the company’s products or make every business decision.

Why do companies sell shares?

A company may sell shares to raise money for things like building products, opening locations, or developing services. Public companies have shares that can be bought and sold by investors.

How might a shareholder benefit?

If a company does well, its share price may rise, and some companies pay dividends. Neither outcome is guaranteed: a share price can also fall, and a company may not pay dividends.

Remember A stock is an ownership share. Its value can rise or fall, so a profit is never guaranteed.
