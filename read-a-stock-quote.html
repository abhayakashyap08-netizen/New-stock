How to read a stock quote

A stock quote is a quick summary of a stock’s trading information.

Ticker symbol and price

A ticker is a short symbol used to identify a company’s shares on a market. The displayed price is the latest reported trading price, and may change while the market is open.

Daily change

A quote may show the amount or percentage the price moved during the day. A green positive change or red negative change describes a past movement; it does not predict what happens next.

Volume

Volume is the number of shares traded during a chosen period. Higher volume means more shares changed hands, but it does not by itself tell you whether a stock is a good investment.

Remember Quotes describe market activity. They are a snapshot, not a promise about future performance.
