How stock trading works

A stock trade connects someone who wants to buy shares with someone who wants to sell them.

The basic path

An investor usually places an order through a broker. The broker sends the order to a market or exchange, where it can be matched with a seller. The final price depends on available buyers and sellers.

Market orders

A market order asks to buy or sell as soon as possible at the best available price. The exact price can change before the order is completed, especially in a fast-moving market.

Limit orders

A limit order sets the highest price a buyer is willing to pay, or the lowest price a seller is willing to accept. It may not complete if the market does not reach that price.

Remember Orders are instructions. A market order prioritizes speed; a limit order sets a price boundary but may not fill.
